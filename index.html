The shift

Firms are racing to clone their best people. The clone belongs to the firm.

AI twins of employees are already live inside law firms and banks, trained on the employee's emails, documents and judgment. That's useful for the firm. But when the employee leaves, the twin stays. Ints turns that around.

Nov 2026 · Proof

KPMG's AI agents out-questioned its own auditors, and a human still had to sign. More than 100 agents ran a full audit of a FTSE 250 company and, in places, "went so much deeper" than KPMG's team. How it works

Verify. Train. Sign.

Three steps between your experience and an agent you can put your name to.

  1. 01

    Verify

    Your employers, roles and qualifications are checked independently by our team. What's verified decides what your Int is allowed to do.

  2. 02

    Train

    Your Int learns from your own templates, style and past work, privately. It writes like you because it learned from you, and nobody else's agent learns from your work.

  3. 03

    Sign

    Your Int drafts, and you review, refine and sign. Nothing reaches a client without a verified human's name on it.
